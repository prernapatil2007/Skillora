# Skillora --- Landing page

**Build Skills. Shape Your Future.**

Skillora is a modern, responsive landing page for an online learning platform. It helps learners pick up coding, UI/UX design, Python, and other career-ready skills through practical, project-based courses.

---

## Features

- **Clean, modern UI** with an indigo/purple theme and rounded card design
- **Sticky navigation bar** with Home, Courses, About Us, and Contact links
- **Hero section** with a bold headline and a clear call to action (*Explore Courses*)
- **Hero image** showing a real learning setup (laptop, notebook, headphones)
- **"Why Choose Skillora" section** with three highlight cards:
  - **Expert-Led Courses**: learn from industry experts and experienced professionals
  - **Learn at Your Pace**: study anytime, anywhere, with flexible learning
  - **Career-Ready Projects**: build real-world projects and showcase your skills
- **Footer** with Contact, Privacy Policy, and Terms links
- **Fully responsive** layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile

---

## Page Structure

| Section | Description |

## Design Notes

- **Primary color:** Indigo / violet (`#4F46E5` range)
- **Heading text:** Dark navy
- **Background:** Soft off-white / light lavender
- **Typography:** Poppins (bold headings, regular body text)
- **Components:** Rounded buttons, rounded image, and cards with soft borders
